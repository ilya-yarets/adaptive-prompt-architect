# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss ideas and observable behavior rather than attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of another person's private data are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and users. Use GitHub's reporting and blocking tools for conduct concerns. Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
